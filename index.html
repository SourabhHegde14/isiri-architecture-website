Welcome
to iSiri

iSiri is a multidisciplinary design practice founded by Ar Soumya HR in 2004, based in Bengaluru. We approach design as a journey of discovery, matching built spaces closely to the people who use them to create high-quality, responsive and responsible work.

Attention to detail, function, form and balance defines how we work. By reimagining what a space can do, we create environments for modern living that bring together sustainability, functionality and aesthetic appeal.

Principal Architect & Founder

Ar Soumya HR

A graduate of BVBCET, Hubli, Soumya began her career with leading architectural firms in Bengaluru. Over more than 20 years she has worked across architecture, interior design, project management and landscape architecture.

Her interest in experimental techniques and a deep understanding of design aesthetics led her to establish iSiri in 2004. A skilled artist, she brings a strong eye, a close focus on detailing and a commitment to work made for each client.

No space is too small to be designed. Creativity has no size or limits.
